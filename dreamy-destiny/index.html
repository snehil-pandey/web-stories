The Girl

Confident enough to know what she wants. Shy enough to forget how to say it when he's looking at her.

Story Overview

A multi-chapter story covering the days immediately before their wedding.
The romance will stay sweet, fluttery and intimate, rather than becoming dramatic for the sake of drama.
Muse's feelings will mostly be shown through inner thoughts, tiny reactions, awkward decisions and things he notices but never says.
Sylvia's side will carry more dialogue because she is the clearer, more direct communicator.
The reader should often understand something about Sylvia before Muse does.
The next development should not be predictable. Small misunderstandings, unexpected meetings, work interruptions, family moments, and little discoveries will keep changing the rhythm.
